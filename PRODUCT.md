# Product

> Design-tool context only. The authoritative requirements remain the documents listed in `AGENTS.md`; they override this summary if any wording differs.

## Register

product

## Users

Clinic owners and administrators, dentists, receptionists, dental assistants, and visiting or on-call specialists working in Philippine dental clinics. They use the private application during focused, time-sensitive clinical and operational workflows across desktop, iPad/tablet, and mobile devices. Patients use separate public surfaces for clinic information and appointment-related flows.

## Product Purpose

Provide a secure, dental-first EMR and practice-management foundation that replaces fragmented paper and manual workflows while preserving printable workflows. The product should make organization and branch context clear, keep operational tasks efficient, and establish safe multi-tenant patterns for future SaaS use.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical, and modern. The interface should communicate trust, clarity, cleanliness, professional healthcare, and operational confidence without feeling sterile or generic.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic generated SaaS dashboard, a consumer social app, or an over-decorated healthcare theme. Avoid greeting heroes, mandatory KPI-card rows, decorative charts, tile galleries for transactional work, glassmorphism, gradients, neon or rainbow palettes, excessive rounding and shadows, marketing-scale whitespace, hover-only controls, and large decorative illustrations inside the EMR.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the current organization, branch, patient, or appointment context explicit whenever a wrong-context action would matter.
2. Favor compact, task-shaped work surfaces over decorative dashboard composition.
3. Use familiar controls and predictable action placement so the interface disappears into the clinic workflow.
4. Treat the browser as an interaction layer; authorization and clinical truth remain server and database responsibilities.
5. Adapt structure and density intentionally for touch, keyboard, tablet, and phone use without hiding important meaning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA wherever practical. Provide semantic labels and landmarks, visible keyboard focus, sufficient text contrast, non-color state cues, reduced-motion support, accessible overlays and errors, touch-friendly controls, and no hover-only or drag-only critical interaction. Clinical meaning must never exist only in a visual representation.
